# Security policy

Found a security hole in Zaisma? Tell us privately, and you won't get in trouble for looking. The full policy is at [zaisma.com/legal/security](https://zaisma.com/legal/security).

## How to report

Email [security@zaisma.com](mailto:security@zaisma.com), or [report it privately on GitHub](https://github.com/zaisma/zaisma/security/advisories/new). Say where it is, how to make it happen, and what it lets someone do. Please don't open a public issue or pull request for a hole.

## What's covered

- zaisma.com, its API and its MCP.
- Your own Zaisma account and your own games.
- The game server, the SDK and the upload command in this repo.

## Safe harbor

If you look for holes in good faith and stay inside the rules below, we won't take legal action against you and we won't close your account.

## The rules

- Test only on your own account and your own games. If you reach another player's account or data, stop at the smallest proof, don't keep it, and tell us.
- No denial of service, and nothing that slows Zaisma for other players.
- No spam, phishing or tricking other players.
- Never upload illegal content, even to prove a point. Child sexual abuse material has no safe harbor: it's reported and passed to the police.
- Give us 90 days to fix a hole before you publish it.

## What to expect

We reply within 5 days, and aim to fix serious holes within 30. We tell you when it's fixed. There's no cash reward yet.
