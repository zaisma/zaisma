1. Arachnid Shield Must first, about 10 minutes

A free service from the Canadian Centre for Child Protection that checks images against known child abuse images. Zaisma will run it on every image in every upload before a game goes live.

  1. Go to projectarachnid.ca/en/api/accounts/register and create an account.
  2. Email: safety@zaisma.com. Organization: Zaisma LLC. Website: zaisma.com.
  3. Ask Julius for the confirmation email, then sign in.
  4. Once you're signed in, the site gives you an API key, or a username and password for the API. Send it to Julius privately: a password manager share or something similar, never chat or email. He puts it in Cloudflare's settings.

If they ask for more detail or want to talk first, answer from the text above. Questions for them go through their contact page. Their technical docs are at shield.projectarachnid.com/docs, for Julius and Claude, not you.

2. Register with NCMEC Must start now, they may take a while to approve

US law requires services that host user uploads to register with NCMEC's CyberTipline, which is how we report child abuse material if we ever find any. It's free.

  1. Go to esp.ncmec.org/registration and register Zaisma LLC as an electronic service provider (ESP).
  2. Email: safety@zaisma.com. Ask Julius for any confirmation email.
  3. They'll ask for the company name, address, phone, an emergency contact, and what the service does. Use the company details above. Julius is the emergency contact; ask him for his number.
  4. If you get stuck or they have questions, write to ESPteam@ncmec.org.
  5. Send Julius the login details for the ESP account privately (a password manager share, never chat or email). Zaisma will use them to send reports automatically.

3. Register the DMCA agent Must about 15 minutes, $6

A listing at the US Copyright Office naming who receives copyright takedown notices for Zaisma. Without it, Zaisma isn't protected when someone uploads a copied game.

  1. Go to copyright.gov/dmca-directory and click Login/Register. Make the account with dmca@zaisma.com.
  2. Service provider: Zaisma LLC, at the company street address (no P.O. box).
  3. Alternate names: add Zaisma, zaisma.com and zaismausercontent.com.
  4. Designated agent: name Copyright Agent (a role is allowed, so no one's personal name goes on the record), organization Zaisma LLC, the company address, the company phone, email dmca@zaisma.com.
  5. Administrative contact: email dmca@zaisma.com, so renewal reminders reach Julius. Your own name is fine as the person.
  6. Pay the $6 fee. Ask Julius which card to use.
  7. Send Julius the confirmation, or a screenshot of the finished listing. We'll put the agent details on zaisma.com/dmca; you don't need to change the site.

The listing expires after 3 years. Renewing costs another $6, and the Copyright Office emails reminders to dmca@ ahead of time.
